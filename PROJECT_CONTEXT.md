# Bhola Sofa - Project & Business Context

## Business Overview
* **Brand Name:** Bhola Sofa Manufacturers
* **Location:** Jugsalai, Jamshedpur, Jharkhand
* **Legacy:** Established in 1978 by the late Mr. MD Aslam. 
* **Current Leadership:** Mr. Anwar (Mabu)
* **Core Philosophy:** 100% custom-made furniture, factory-direct wholesale pricing. The brand relies on authentic craftsmanship and local trust, strictly avoiding generic "imported" or "international" marketing fluff.

## The Mission & Family Context
This project is driven by a deeply personal mission. The website and digital strategy were built entirely for free by a family member (the User) to support Mr. Anwar. 
* **The Situation:** Mr. Anwar is working to scale the original 1978 family legacy. While his brothers have found their own success, they are not actively helping him scale or modernize this business. 
* **The Goal:** To equip Mr. Anwar with enterprise-grade digital tools (React SPA, AI chatbots, programmatic SEO) to completely dominate the local Jamshedpur market, outpace competitors, and ensure the grandfather's legacy not only survives but thrives.

## Technical Architecture
* **Frontend:** React.js (Vite Single Page Application)
* **Styling:** Custom CSS with a premium gold/navy color palette (No generic Tailwind).
* **Backend/Database:** Supabase (for dynamic product catalog retrieval).
* **State Management:** React Context (`CartContext`, `WishlistContext`).
* **AI Integration:** System-prompted Gemini/AI Chatbot embedded directly into the UI, trained specifically on Bhola Sofa's local delivery rules and custom manufacturing capabilities.

## E-Commerce & Delivery Logic (3-Zone System)
Because custom furniture requires high-touch sales, there is no traditional checkout. The cart redirects to a formatted WhatsApp message to close the deal.
* **Zone 1 (Jamshedpur/Adityapur - Free):** Pincodes starting with 831 or 832.
* **Zone 2 (Jharkhand - Paid Freight):** Pincodes starting with 81, 82, or 83 (excluding Zone 1). Customer is warned about freight charges on WhatsApp.
* **Zone 3 (Outside Jharkhand - Blocked):** All other pincodes are politely declined to maintain local quality control.

## SEO & Marketing Strategy
* **Topical Authority:** A custom blog system featuring 20 highly-targeted articles specifically addressing Jamshedpur climate, local real estate (e.g., small flats in Adityapur), and furniture comparisons.
* **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization):** Heavy use of JSON-LD Schema markup and structured Q&A formats to feed exact facts to Google's AI Overviews.
* **Local Dominance:** Integrated with Google My Business (pending phone number verification) to dominate the "near me" Map Pack.
